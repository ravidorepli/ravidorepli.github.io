shorts-bot privacy policy

shorts-bot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

shorts-bot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled through YouTube API Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data

It uses the YouTube upload permission solely to upload the owner's videos. It does not collect, store, share or sell data about any other person. Access tokens are kept as private secrets by the owner and are not disclosed to anyone. No YouTube data is retained beyond the uploads themselves.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
